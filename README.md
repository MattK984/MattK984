### Matthias Knauth: AI automations for settings where mistakes are expensive

I build and run AI agents for regulated environments: banks, fintechs, digital-asset firms and critical infrastructure. The part I care about is what makes them survive a risk review: human approval before actions, an audit trail, evals that run on every change, and a known cost per completed task.

**Background:** 20+ years in banking, trading and digital assets (Dresdner Bank, Commerzbank, Head of Trading at First Coin, Head of Product at Vana). Today I build on-prem LLM and agent systems for KRITIS-regulated operators under NIS2, IEC 62443 and the EU AI Act.

**Repos**
- [`agent-ops-lab`](https://github.com/MattK984/agent-ops-lab): LangGraph automations in production on a small self-run server, with guardrails, tracing and evals in LangSmith. Built in the open, cycle by cycle.
- **Trading agent** (private repo): a local multi-agent crypto research and paper-trading system. Deterministic backtests, bounded LLM-assisted research, pre-registered hypotheses, locked holdouts, risk gates and provenance. No live capital; its first alpha study ended with a negative verdict, and the hypothesis was retired rather than tuned.
- [`ot-ai-compliance-map`](https://github.com/MattK984/ot-ai-compliance-map): obligations from the EU AI Act, NIS2 and IEC 62443 mapped to concrete AI deployment scenarios.

**Stack:** LangGraph · LangSmith · Python · Postgres · Docker
